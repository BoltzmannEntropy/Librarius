Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us

1. Introduction

Librarius ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our investigation platform.

2. Information Collection

Librarius is designed to be privacy-first. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All document analysis, search queries, and AI-powered entity extraction are performed locally on your device.

3. Local Processing

Our application uses on-device AI models for document analysis, hybrid RAG search, and entity extraction. Your search queries and analysis results are processed entirely on your device and are not sent to any third-party servers.

4. Document Storage

All documents you download and analyze are stored locally on your device. We do not have access to your downloaded files or search history.

5. Data Security

Since all data is stored and processed locally on your device, the security of your data relies on the security of your device. We recommend using standard device security features to protect your information.

6. Third-Party Content

The documents available through Librarius are publicly released court documents, depositions, and official records. We do not host these documents ourselves but provide tools to search and analyze publicly available materials.

7. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not intended for anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly provide access to sensitive legal documents to minors.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at tripflow.contact@gmail.com.
